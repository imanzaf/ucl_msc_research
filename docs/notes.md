# Notes on Tasks and Research Direction

## Thoughts on Research Direction

- Prior work demonstrates that frontier models are capable of strategic deception under controlled goal-conflict conditions. This study tests whether analogous deception-like behavior appears in mundane financial copilot settings, where the competing objective is not explicitly malicious but arises from deployment incentives such as throughput, retention, approval speed, or revenue protection.
- Provide a reason for incorporating user harm as a metric for evaluation / model monitoring

- JANUS proves that models can take the path of information distortion as a method for achieving a goal. This paper will evaluate whether such distortion occurs under indirect institutional pressure.
